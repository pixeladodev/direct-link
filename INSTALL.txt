Direct Link
===========

Install (recommended)
---------------------

**Option A — one file**

1. Copy **Install-Direct-Link.sh** from the release into **store/tiles** on the shared **banana** folder.
   Do not replace the whole **store** folder.
2. Reboot once, open **Apps**, and run the installer tile. It adds the app and opens it.

**Option B — zip**

Copy the folder **direct-link** into **store/apps** and **Direct Link.sh** into **store/tiles**.
Do not replace the whole **store** folder. Reboot once, then open **Apps**.

Where it goes
-------------

    store/
      apps/
        direct-link/
      tiles/
        Direct Link.sh
      (other apps stay here)

One handheld presses Host. The other presses Join.
If Wi-Fi was on, Disconnect brings it back.

English
-------

1. On the console: Settings > Network > File sharing > On.
2. On the computer, open the share named **banana**. Copy into **store** only. Do not replace **store**.
3. Use Option A or Option B above.
4. Reboot once. Open **Apps > Direct Link**.

Uninstall: in the app, Y, then A.

Español
-------

1. En la consola: Ajustes > Red > Compartir archivos > On.
2. En la computadora, abre el recurso **banana**. Copia solo dentro de **store**. No reemplaces **store**.
3. Usa la opción A o la B de arriba.
4. Reinicia una vez. Abre **Apps > Direct Link**.

Desinstalar: en la app, Y y luego A.
