# Direct Link 0.1.0

**Direct Link** is an Apps-shelf app for BananaOS that puts two handhelds on their own Wi-Fi link, so local
multiplayer works **without a router**. Made for **Pixelado Party** and any other app with LAN play.

**Devices:** Anbernic **RG DS** and **RG DS Plus**, BananaOS **0.5.3.6**.

### What it does

- **Host or Join in two taps.** Open the app, tap **Host** twice on one handheld and **Join** twice on the other
  (or pick with the d-pad and press **A**).
- **See who is connected.** The top screen says whether the other handheld is there and shows its address; the host
  counts every guest that answers.
- **Cancel** a start or a join while it runs (**B**).
- **Disconnect** with **X**: your home Wi-Fi comes back.
- **Picks the quietest channel** (1, 6 or 11) where many routers are around.
- **No drops from its own checks:** the link is only rebuilt when the other handheld is really gone.
- **The link stays up** after you leave the app, so you can play several games in a row.
- **English or Spanish**, following the handheld's language. Pixelado's own pixel-art look on any theme, with Nilo
  and Lumi keeping you company.

### Install

**Option A — one file (recommended):**

1. Download **`Install-Direct-Link.sh`** from this release.
2. Copy only that file into **`store/tiles`** on the shared **banana** folder. Do **not** replace the whole `store`
   folder.
3. Reboot once, open **Apps**, and run the installer tile. Then open **Direct Link** from its new tile.

**Option B — zip:** extract it; copy **`direct-link`** into **`store/apps`** and **`Direct Link.sh`** into
**`store/tiles`**; reboot once.

If you used the earlier **bananaos-direct-link**, this replaces it in the same place; your settings stay.

### Use it

1. On both handhelds: open **Direct Link**.
2. On one: **Host**. The top screen shows **Waiting…** until the other one is there.
3. On the other: **Join**. Both show **Connected** within a few seconds (if Join was started first, it can take up
   to about a minute).
4. Leave the app and open **Pixelado Party** (or another app with LAN play) on both.
5. When you are done: open Direct Link and press **X** to disconnect.

### Tested

- RG DS and RG DS Plus on BananaOS 0.5.3.6, this build: host and guest (each way round, and Join started before
  Host), cancel while starting, the connected count on the host, disconnect.
- Long multiplayer sessions with Pixelado Party over the link, with an earlier build of the same link.
