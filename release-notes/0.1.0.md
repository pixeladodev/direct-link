**Direct Link** puts two handhelds on their own Wi-Fi link, so local multiplayer works **without a router**: at home for a steadier connection, and away from home too. Made for **[Pixelado Party](https://github.com/pixeladodev/pixelado-party)** and any other app with LAN play.

**Devices:** Anbernic **RG DS** and **RG DS Plus**, BananaOS **0.5.3.6**.

### Highlights

- **Host or Join in two taps**, with the d-pad or the touch screen.
- **See who is connected** on the top screen; **B** cancels, **X** disconnects and brings your home Wi-Fi back.
- **Picks the quietest channel** (1, 6 or 11) and keeps the link up after you leave the app, so you can play several games in a row.
- **English or Spanish**, in Pixelado's pixel-art look, with Nilo and Lumi keeping you company.

### Install

1. Download **`Install-Direct-Link.sh`** from this release.
2. Copy only that file into **`store/tiles`** on the shared **banana** folder.
3. Reboot once, open **Apps**, run the installer tile, then open **Direct Link**.

### Use it

1. Open **Direct Link** on both handhelds: **Host** on one, **Join** on the other. Both show **Connected** within a few seconds.
2. Leave the app and open **Pixelado Party** (or another app with LAN play) on both.
3. When you are done, open Direct Link and press **X**.
